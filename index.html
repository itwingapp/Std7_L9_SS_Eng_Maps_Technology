Maps and Technology to Know the Earth

Why Maps?

Maps help us locate places, find routes, and understand geographical features.

Printed maps → Digital maps on phones & vehicles

Planning a trip with a map saves time and makes the journey enjoyable.

Globe vs Map

Globe: Spherical model of Earth; shows true shape; latitudes/longitudes accurate; hard to carry.

Map: Flat representation; easy to carry; may have some distortion; many types for many uses.

Types of Maps

Physical maps — natural features (soil, rivers, climate, vegetation, topography)
Cultural maps — man-made features (political divisions, roads, population, economy)

Digital Maps

Maps on mobile phones and vehicles. Help find places and routes easily (e.g. Meeshappulimalai trip).

Updated often; zoom; turn-by-turn navigation.

Technology & GPS

Satellites and GPS support digital mapping and location services.

Technology helps us know the Earth better — from printed maps to satellite images.

Map Elements

Title, scale, direction (north arrow), legend/symbols, colours — help read a map correctly.

Function of a map is related to its content and title.

Source of information; locate, route, features; printed to digital.
